03 / THE OPEN LEDGER

Good intentions deserve good records.

JustADrop began with a simple thought: save a little, pool it together, and make someone's day better. This ledger is how we keep that promise visible — not just at the moment of giving, but all the way through to how the money is used.

We publish only records that have been reconciled. Personal payment details and sensitive beneficiary information are never exposed.
