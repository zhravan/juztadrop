03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Our contributions. Our responsibility.

We collect savings every month and utilise them for social services and requirements. This public ledger extends that original promise: every contribution should be accounted for, every expense should have a purpose, and our community should be able to see how shared savings are used.

We publish reconciled financial records while protecting private payer details and sensitive beneficiary information.
