GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Who can volunteer?

Anyone willing to give their time, care and effort to a cause can get involved. You do not need to be part of an organisation to reach out and learn about ways to help.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Many activities simply need people who are dependable and willing to participate. If an activity requires specialist skills or experience, that will be communicated before you take it on.

Can I volunteer on weekends?

Yes, where weekend activities are available. Timing depends on the initiative and the organisation coordinating it. Get in touch and let us know when you are available.

Can I support without contributing money?

Absolutely. You can contribute your time, skills, effort or simply help spread the word. Financial contributions are not a requirement for volunteering.

How are organisations and requests verified?

We review the information shared with us and speak with the people coordinating an initiative before circulating it. If you need more details about a specific request, contact us before participating or contributing.

Where can I see how contributions are used?

Our Transparency page will publish integrated contribution and expense records from December 2026, following the introduction of our payment app integration. Before then, contributions and expenses were recorded manually, without an integrated system. Earlier records may not appear in the online ledger. We will distinguish historical manual records from entries recorded through the new system.
