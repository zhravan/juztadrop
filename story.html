OUR STORY

A little help, shared by many.

Just-a-Drop is a community initiative built around a simple idea: small contributions can make a meaningful difference when we come together.

Start small

We encourage people to set aside ₹1 a day. That adds up to ₹30 a month, and every contribution matters.

Act together

We bring people together to support community needs, local initiatives and fundraisers.

Stay involved

Contribute, volunteer or help connect someone who needs support with the community.
